# Motes 2.0 — Choreographed Emergence

**Date:** 2026-07-03
**Status:** Draft for review
**Supersedes:** incremental evolution of 1.x (log entries through 2026-04-02)

---

## 1. Why 2.0

A full-cycle audit (project's own capture + analyzer tooling, 2026-07-03) found:

- The world runs at **1–3 living motes** (cap 8 since commit `772d1fc`); the analyzer
  measured **0 visible motes at organization** and a **clumping ratio of 0.00 for the
  entire cycle**. No bond, cluster, or settlement has formed since the last tuning pass.
- Everything downstream keys off social phenomena that never occur: 56 evolution-log
  entries of bond/cluster polish decorate nothing; the sound engine's melodic layer is
  driven by `findClusters()` and therefore never plays; events are text banners over an
  empty stage; the phase arc peaks at genesis and dies by mid-cycle — inverted.
- `analyze-quality.mjs` reported *"No quality issues detected"* on that empty world.
  The autonomous evolution loop trusted it and spent months polishing atmosphere.
- Nothing persists between cycles, so day five looks like day one.

The atmosphere (skies, biomes, weather, lighting) is already good. The subject is missing.

## 2. Thesis

**Stop hoping for emergence. Choreograph the skeleton; let emergence fill the flesh.**

Every 5-minute cycle tells one guaranteed story — scattered birth, first meetings, a
procession to a gathering place, a settlement that grows and sings, decline, a last
survivor among ruins — and the *details* (who, where, what biome, what weather, what
melody, what disaster) vary deterministically per cycle. Rarely, deterministically, a
cycle is a tragedy and the colony fails — and everyone on Earth watching that cycle sees
the same tragedy.

One sentence: **a world where community reliably happens, time leaves scars, and the
music is the community made audible.**

## 3. Non-negotiables (unchanged from 1.x)

- 256×144 canvas, nearest-neighbor upscale
- 300-second UTC-synchronized cycles; same cycle number ⇒ same world for everyone
- All simulation randomness through seeded `rng()`; determinism is sacred
- Zero npm runtime dependencies (browser APIs unrestricted)
- The six-phase structure (genesis → exploration → organization → complexity →
  dissolution → silence) and its boundaries

## 4. Approaches considered

| | Approach | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Repair 1.x** — revert population, retune constants | Restores dots, still gambles on emergence. Rejected. |
| B | **Choreographed emergence** — guaranteed arc + memory + sound coupling within sacred constraints | **Chosen.** Transforms the experience; reuses ~80% of engine. |
| C | **Full reimagining** — WebGL world, camera, larger canvas | Breaks identity and constraints; months of work. Rejected. |

## 5. The experience — one cycle, beat by beat

Phase boundaries are the existing ones (10% / 30% / 55% / 80% / 92% / 100%).

| Phase | Story beat | What you see | What you hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Genesis** (0–30s) | Scattered birth | Dark world. Motes wink in one by one across the whole terrain, each with a birth glimmer. Ruins of past cycles visible as silhouettes. | Near-silence. The first mote "hums" a 2-note fragment of this cycle's motif. Sparse birth pings. |
| **Exploration** (30–90s) | First meetings | Motes wander with visible intent (gait per temperament, fading trails). When two meet: bond starburst, blended-color link. | Each new bond adds a sustained interval voice. The soundscape audibly *assembles*. |
| **Organization** (90–165s) | The pull | Drives shift toward the gathering site. Chains of motes travel together — processions crossing hills and water. Site marker appears. | Rhythm emerges: procession footsteps as soft percussion; voices converge toward the motif key. |
| **Complexity** (165–240s) | The settlement sings | Structures grow at the site (camp → hall → beacon, biome-styled). Motes orbit, dance, build. Beacon lights. Peak population, peak light. | Full statement of the cycle motif — choir pad, bond voices, cluster heartbeat. The loudest, richest 60 seconds. |
| **Dissolution** (240–276s) | Decline | Elders die (spirit ascension, visible grief ripples). Structures dim. Rain/leaves per biome. Population falls. | Voices drop out one by one as their motes die. Motif detunes, slows. |
| **Silence** (276–300s) | The last survivor | One mote — deterministically the hardiest — walks among the ruins. Final breath: a rising spirit. World fades. Ruins remain for future cycles. | Solo reprise of the motif, single voice, then one long decaying bell. |

**North-star milestones this must satisfy** (from `.claude/daily-evolve.md`):
3s — something alive and beautiful (a populated, moving world). 30s — distinguishable
individuals and relationships (trails, gaits, bond events). 2min — the arc is felt
(the pull/procession is unmistakable). 5min — a complete story with an ending that
lands (last survivor). Days — ruins accumulate, motifs differ, rare tragedies and
events become lore.

## 6. Systems design

### 6.1 Population & bonding (fix the collapse)

Replace `PHASE_PARAMS` (src/constants.ts:120–125):

| Phase | spawnRate | maxMotes | energyDecay | bondStrength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| genesis | 2.0 | 10 | 0.004 | 0.2 |
| exploration | 1.5 | 24 | 0.008 | 0.7 |
| organization | 0.8 | 32 | 0.010 | 0.9 |
| complexity | 0.3 | 36 | 0.012 | 1.0 |
| dissolution | 0 | 36 | 0.035 | 0.4 |
| silence | 0 | 36 | 0.06 | 0.1 |

- Target: population rises through the first half, **peaks at 28–36 during complexity**,
  then falls. The curve *is* the arc; the analyzer asserts its shape (§9).
- **Compatibility gates color, not existence.** 1.x's compatibility gating + rejection
  behavior made bonds nearly impossible at low density. 2.0: any two motes within bond
  radius for >1.5s bond; compatibility determines bond *character* (warm/cool link color,
  harmony vs. dissonant interval voice, break probability under stress).
- Bond radius raised so encounters convert (tune to make first bond reliably occur
  before 25% progress; start at 10px).
- Energy: standing near bonded companions and inside the settlement slows decay
  (community is literally life-sustaining — this closes the starvation loop that
  contributed to the collapse).

### 6.2 The gathering site & arc controller (new: `src/colony.ts`)

- `chooseSite(terrain, rng): {x, y}` — scores terrain basins on flatness, elevation,
  and water proximity; picked once at cycle start. **Pure function of the cycle seed**,
  which is what makes cross-cycle memory cheap (§6.4).
- New mote drive **belonging**, joining comfort/curiosity/togetherness in the existing
  winner-take-all drive system. Its weight ramps with `cycleProgress`: ~0 during
  genesis/exploration (motes roam freely), dominant by mid-organization (the pull).
  High-wanderlust motes resist longest — stragglers and latecomers are the texture.
- **Processions**: motes en route to the site within 8px of each other link into
  travel chains (leader/follower, single-file over terrain). Rendered as caravans —
  the signature shareable visual of the middle game.
- The arc controller emits **milestone events** (first bond, first procession,
  settlement stage-ups, first death, last survivor) consumed by narrative, sound,
  and effects. One source of truth for "what just happened."

### 6.3 Settlement & structures

- Settlement at the site grows through occupancy-time: **stage 0** ground marker →
  **stage 1** camp (2–3 shelters) → **stage 2** hall → **stage 3** beacon (lit pillar,
  visible at night phases). Sprites ≤ 12×10 px, one set per biome (desert adobe,
  tundra ice-block, volcanic obsidian, lush treehouse, temperate timber): 5 biomes ×
  4 stages, drawn in `terrain-render.ts` layer order (behind motes, in front of terrain).
- Stage-ups are *moments*: build flash, dust, a chord in the sound engine, a narrative line.
- Deaths near the settlement leave **cairns** (2×3 px stone markers) that persist for
  the rest of the cycle and feed §6.4.

### 6.4 Memory — the world remembers (new: `src/history.ts`)

The core trick: because site selection, events, and tragedy are pure functions of the
cycle seed, **any past cycle's outcome is computable without simulating it**.

- `cycleOutcome(cycleNumber): {siteX, siteY, biome, settlementStage, tragedy, eventScar}` —
  cheap pure function (terrain gen for that seed + event/tragedy determination). No
  full simulation, no storage, no server: every viewer derives identical history.
- At cycle start, render the previous **7 cycles'** outcomes as terrain features:
  ruins at their sites (stage-appropriate, progressively eroded with age — full
  silhouette at k−1, rubble at k−7), meteor craters, flood waterlines. Current-cycle
  terrain wins conflicts; ruins clamp to current surface height.
- The live simulation must **honor the derived outcome**: settlementStage from
  `cycleOutcome(current)` is the stage the arc controller drives toward, so the live
  cycle and the future-history view of it always agree.
- Effect: a viewer returning across a day watches a valley accumulate history.
  Determinism preserved; "same world for everyone" now includes the same past.

### 6.5 Sound — the community made audible

- **Cycle motif**: 5–8 note phrase generated from the cycle seed over the biome's
  scale (new: `src/motif.ts`). Every cycle has a signature melody. Fragment at genesis,
  full statement at complexity, solo reprise at silence. ("Silver Canyon's song.")
- **Bond voices**: each living bond contributes one sustained interval voice (capped
  at existing 8-voice polyphony; strongest bonds win slots). Bonds forming/breaking
  audibly add/remove voices — the soundscape literally assembles as the community does.
- **Settlement choir**: stage ≥ 2 adds the choir pad; the beacon adds a low anchor tone.
- **Deaths** silence their voice with a decaying bell (existing lifecycle sounds rewired
  to milestones).
- **Visible sound**: every triggered note pulses its source (mote ring, structure
  shimmer) so muted viewers still perceive rhythm. Audio prompt stays; page copy sells
  headphones before the click.
- Keeps the existing engine, biome profiles, and scales (`sound-config.ts`); replaces the
  cluster-polling voice allocation in `sound.ts:301` with milestone/bond-driven allocation.

### 6.6 Events with teeth & tragedy cycles

- Every event must have **visible consequences on the population and terrain**:
  - *Plague*: contagion spreads visibly mote-to-mote (sickly tint), kills; survivors carry scars.
  - *Meteor*: crater (persists via §6.4), kills in radius, terror scatter, firelight.
  - *Flood*: rising waterline drowns lowlands, forces emergency procession to high ground; if the site floods, the colony rebuilds on a hill (stage regression).
  - *Aurora/Bloom*: energy surge — spawn burst, dance behavior, brightened bonds.
  - *Eclipse*: darkness, motes as the only light, settlement beacon becomes the focal point.
- **Tragedy cycles**: `cycleOutcome()` marks ~1 in 25 cycles as failures — the event
  overwhelms the colony; the settlement never passes stage 1; the population dies out
  before silence begins; no last-survivor reprise, just wind over cairns.
  Deterministic, shared, rare: lore.
- Event banner fixed: honors `messageAlpha` timing verified at 1× speed; banner never
  parks over the sky (current opaque band replaced by letterbox + short title only).

### 6.7 Narrative — a truthful observer

- All narrative lines become **milestone-driven** (from the arc controller) and
  state-aware; ambient filler only when nothing has happened for >20s. No more
  "thriving in the impossible cold" over an empty desert.
- The **elder** (oldest living mote) and the **last survivor** get procedurally
  generated names (extend `names.ts`); milestone lines reference them
  ("Oru reaches the gathering place"). Attachment needs a name.
- Existing 3–8 word brevity rule stands.

### 6.8 Rendering & composition

- **Figure-ground guarantee**: cap sky luminance behind the terrain band so motes and
  structures always meet a minimum contrast (the desert sun currently flattens the
  frame). Sun halo shrinks near horizon.
- **Terrain occupancy**: raise heightmap amplitude/variance so the living band spans
  more of the frame; site basins sit near the golden-ratio focal line, not the floor.
- **Emissive glow pass**: second 256×144 emissive buffer (motes, bonds, beacon, spirits
  write to it), one cheap separable blur, additive composite before upscale. Canvas 2D
  only. This replaces ad-hoc per-sprite glow loops and makes night phases luminous.
- **Trails**: short fading position trails per mote (render-motes), giving 3px creatures
  motion identity.
- Bug fixes: cursor force-field ring only renders after real pointer presence;
  chromatic aberration stays transition-only (verified at 1×).

### 6.9 Interaction

Unchanged mechanics (hover attracts, swipe scatters, click energizes) — but against
crowds and processions they finally have drama: scatter a caravan, lure a straggler,
energize a builder. Interaction remains local-only perturbation (accepted 1.x stance
on determinism).

## 7. Architecture

New modules:

```
src/colony.ts     # site selection, belonging drive input, arc controller, milestones
src/history.ts    # cycleOutcome() pure fn; ruins/crater derivation for past cycles
src/motif.ts      # seeded melody generation over biome scales
```

Touched modules: `constants.ts` (population, bond, site, structure tuning),
`mote.ts` (belonging drive, bond gate change, procession linking),
`world.ts` (arc controller integration, milestone emission),
`terrain-render.ts` (structures, ruins, cairns), `render-motes.ts` (trails, gait),
`render.ts`/`main.ts` (emissive pass), `sound.ts` + `sound-*` (voice allocation, motif),
`narrative.ts` (milestone-driven lines), `render-ui.ts` (banner/cursor fixes),
`names.ts` (mote names), `scripts/analyze-quality.mjs` (hard gates).

Data flow: `world.update()` → `colony.update()` emits milestones →
(narrative, sound, effects) consume; `history.cycleOutcome()` is read-only input to
terrain-gen (ruins) and colony (target stage).

## 8. Determinism guarantees

- `chooseSite`, `cycleOutcome`, motif, event selection, tragedy flag: pure functions of
  cycle number (via seeded rng). No wall-clock, no `Math.random`, no storage.
- Cross-cycle memory derives from seeds, never from recorded live state — so a viewer
  arriving mid-day sees identical ruins to one who watched all day.
- Semantic determinism tests (§9) replace nothing — they're new.

## 9. Instrumentation & quality gates (the gate must be able to fail)

`analyze-quality.mjs` gains hard assertions and **exits non-zero** on violation:

1. Peak population 28–36, occurring within complexity (55–80% progress).
2. Clumping ratio ≥ 0.4 by end of organization (non-tragedy cycles).
3. First bond before 25% progress; settlement stage ≥ 2 by 70% (non-tragedy).
4. Exactly one living mote in the final 4% (the last survivor), zero after cycle
   end (non-tragedy; tragedy cycles assert zero motes by 90%).
5. Mote/backdrop luminance delta ≥ 0.25 (analyzer's existing 0–1 brightness scale)
   in every phase (figure-ground guarantee).
6. Tragedy rate over a 50-cycle sample: 2–6%.

Plus a **determinism check**: run cycle N twice headless; assert identical milestone
timelines and population curves. All gates runnable in CI and required by the daily
evolution loop before commit. `.claude/daily-evolve.md` gains one line: *"if the
analyzer fails, nothing else matters."*

## 10. Milestones (each independently shippable, in order)

- **M0 — Instruments first.** Analyzer hard gates + determinism check (they will fail
  against 1.x; that's the point). Banner/cursor bug fixes.
- **M1 — Repopulation & the pull.** New PHASE_PARAMS, bond gate change, community
  energy sharing, `colony.ts` with site + belonging drive + processions. *Exit: gates
  1–4 pass without structures (stage checks stubbed).*
- **M2 — Settlement & structures.** Biome structure sprites, stage growth, cairns,
  stage-up moments. *Exit: gate 3 fully passes; complexity phase has a focal spectacle.*
- **M3 — Sound rebuild.** Motif, bond-voice allocation, choir, reprise, visible sound.
  *Exit: a muted screen recording still shows rhythm; headphone cycle is a song.*
- **M4 — Memory.** `history.ts`, ruins/craters/waterlines from past 7 cycles.
  *Exit: two consecutive real cycles visibly share history.*
- **M5 — Events with teeth & tragedies.** Consequence pass per event; tragedy cycles.
  *Exit: gate 6; each event visually identifiable with sound muted and text hidden.*
- **M6 — Light & composition.** Emissive glow pass, figure-ground cap, terrain
  occupancy, trails, gaits. *Exit: gate 5; before/after captures pass the 3-second test.*

## 11. Success criteria

- All §9 gates green across a 50-cycle headless sample.
- The five north-star milestones (§5) each map to a shipped, observable feature.
- A 30-second muted screen recording of complexity phase is self-evidently alive
  (the shareability test).
- The daily evolution loop can resume with gates that fail when the world is empty.

## 12. Risks & mitigations

- **Convergence feels scripted.** Belonging ramps gradually; wanderers resist; site
  and route differ per cycle; tragedy breaks the pattern. If it still reads mechanical,
  add noise to arrival timing, never to the destination.
- **36 motes re-crowds the canvas.** The 1.x "individuals" instinct was right at the
  wrong altitude: legibility comes from trails/gait/glow + focal structures, not
  low headcount. Gate 5 plus capture review at every milestone.
- **Ruins clutter terrain gen.** Cap at 7 cycles, erode aggressively, clamp to surface;
  ruins render behind current-cycle features.
- **Sound allocation churn** (bonds forming/breaking rapidly). Hysteresis on voice
  slots (a bond must live 3s to claim a voice, voices release over 2s).
- **Perf** (36 motes + glow pass at 60fps). Glow blur is separable on a 256×144 buffer
  (~110k pixel ops); spatial hash already exists. Budget: frame ≤ 4ms mid-tier laptop;
  measure in M6.

## 13. Out of scope for 2.0

- WebGL pipeline (Canvas 2D emissive pass covers the need; revisit if M6 misses perf).
- Cross-cycle creature survival, lineages, or genetics.
- Server-side anything, viewer counts, chat, or multiplayer presence.
- Canvas size, cycle length, or phase-structure changes.
